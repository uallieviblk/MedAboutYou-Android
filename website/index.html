Know your medicines.
Run your therapy.

Search the European (EMA) and Italian (AIFA) medicine databases and manage your full medication schedule — reminders, a dose log, adherence stats and refill forecasting — all stored only on your phone.

⬇ Download latest APK See how it works

Debug build · ~20 MB · installs on Android 8.0+. For phones, enable “install from unknown sources”.

🔒 Privacy first. Your schedules, dose log and stock never leave the device. No account, no analytics, no cloud sync. The only network use is looking up medicine information; the only optional off-device action is a caregiver SMS you set up yourself.

Everything your routine needs

🔎

Find a medicine

Search EMA (cached, offline) or AIFA (live). Rich records, type badges, official EPAR/RCP links — or add a custom medicine.

🗓️

Schedule anything

Hourly to yearly, multi-time, weekly patterns. End on a date, after N doses, or ongoing.

🏠

Today at a glance

Adherence ring, a time-of-day timeline, refill banner and one-tap “take all”.

📅

Calendar & single-dose edits

Colour-coded month grid; retime or skip one dose, or change this and all following.

💊

Manage therapies

Pause/resume (timed or indefinite), edit from today, cancel without losing history; per-schedule notes.

📈

Insights

7/30/90-day adherence, streaks, a 30-day heatmap, per-medicine bars and upcoming refills.

⏰

Reminders

Exact-alarm notifications with Take/Skip, plus optional caregiver SMS for overdue doses.

📦

Stock & refills

Top up by real pack sizes; the app forecasts when each medicine runs out.

How it works

  1. 1

    Add a medicine from Schedules → Add medicine, then “Add to my medication schedule”.

  2. 2

    Build the schedule — times, recurrence, window and notes.

  3. 3

    Track & review from Today and Insights — reminders keep you on time.
